TimeKeep - sample date conversion (BWQ)

Displays dates in five formats: J2000 seconds (seconds since J2000.0), UTC/G/J (UTC, Gregorian or Julian calendar), Julian Days, ISO week (CAT) (ISO week date with Civil Atomic Time), and Civil Atomic Time (TAI-based atomic time since J2000.0). This data comes with ABSOLUTELY NO WARRANTY.

For the overview of projects see: https://github.com/gxworks/gxworks.github.io/blob/main/overview.html.

Enter a date (e.g. 2002-02-02) and click Use date to include it in the table.

Demonstrates DateFunc time/date conversions from the BWQ Java library, compiled to JS via J2CL.
